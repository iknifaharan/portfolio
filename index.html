02 / About

How I work

I work in the space between what people need and what technology can do.

Over six years, I’ve evolved from UX UI Designer to UX Engineer—shaping products across enterprise SaaS, cloud infrastructure, logistics, utilities, fintech, and mobile platforms.

My route into UX began with engineering. Studying how technical systems work led to a more human question: how should they work for people?
